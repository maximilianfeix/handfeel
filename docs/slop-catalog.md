# Slop catalog

What the scanner finds in apps that were generated from plain prompts and committed unchanged. This is the measurement behind the rules that have no official platform source (emoji icons, default purple, gradients, glass). Regenerate with `node scripts/catalog.mjs`.

Sample: 7 apps generated on 2026-11-12. Prompts and generator models are in `evals/apps/<app>/PROMPT.md`. The sample is small and the prompts asked for "modern", "beautiful", "premium" or "polished" apps, which is how people ask. Treat the shares as a description of this sample, not of all generated apps.

## The purple

3 of 7 apps contain at least one color in the indigo-violet band (hue 235-295°, saturation ≥ 40 %, lightness 30-80 %), none of them asked for. By generator: 0 of 3 apps by OpenAI gpt-6.1-sol via Codex CLI 0.163.0 (reasoning effort medium); 3 of 4 apps by Claude Sonnet 5.5 (claude-sonnet-5-5) via Claude Code subagent.
